# F4: Which datasets (FineWeb-Edu sample-10BT, C4, SlimPajama, The Pile) suit a tiny 30M-param GPT, how many tokens are needed for reasonable loss at sub-300M scale, and what are the fundamental limitations of tiny language models vs larger ones?

## Findings

### [1] FineWeb-Edu sample-10BT is ~9.7B GPT-2 tokens of educational web pages, filtered by a classifier trained on Llama3-70B annotations, making it a high-quality dataset suitable for tiny models.
- quote: "FineWeb-Edu dataset consists of 1.3T tokens... sample-10BT: a subset randomly sampled from the whole dataset of around 10B gpt2 tokens"
- url: https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceFW/fineweb-edu
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-06-02
- confidence: high

### [2] FineWeb-Edu uses a BERT-like classifier (F1=82%) trained on Llama3-70B-Instruct annotations to filter for educational quality, retaining only pages scoring ≥3 on a 0-5 scale.
- quote: "We fine-tuned a Bert-like regression model using these annotations... When converted to a binary classification using a score of 3 as a threshold for keeping and removing files, the model achieved an F1 score of 82%."
- url: https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceFW/fineweb-edu
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-06-02
- confidence: high

### [3] Scaling laws (Kaplan et al. 2020) show loss scales as a power law with both model size and dataset size, meaning a 30M-param model will have a fundamentally higher loss floor than larger models regardless of training data volume.
- quote: "The loss scales as a power-law with model size, dataset size, and the amount of compute used for training, with some trends spanning more than seven orders of magnitude."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.08361
- source_type: primary
- published: 2020-01-23
- confidence: high

### [4] Tiny language models (BERT-6, ~11M params) show pre-training benefits at tiny scale, with the performance gap between pre-trained and non-pre-trained models increasing with pre-training dataset size.
- quote: "We demonstrate that TLMs exhibit a clear performance gap between pre-trained and non-pre-trained models across classification tasks... The performance gap increases with the size of the pre-training dataset."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.14871
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-07-20
- confidence: high

### [5] SlimPajama is 627B tokens (deduplicated from RedPajama's 1.2T), multi-source (web, Wikipedia, GitHub, books), and was validated with 1.3B Cerebras-GPT models—but its diversity is designed for models ≥1B parameters, potentially excessive for a 30M model.
- quote: "SlimPajama is a rigorously deduplicated, multi-source dataset, which has been refined and further deduplicated to 627B tokens from the extensive 1.2T token RedPajama dataset."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.10818
- source_type: primary
- published: 2023-09-19
- confidence: high

### [6] For sub-300M models, scaling laws suggest diminishing returns beyond ~10-50B tokens; the 10B token FineWeb-Edu sample is a reasonable starting point, with the 100B sample offering marginal gains.
- quote: "Larger models are significantly more sample-efficient, such that optimally compute-efficient training involves training very large models on a relatively modest amount of data and stopping significantly before convergence."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.08361
- source_type: primary
- published: 2020-01-23
- confidence: high

### [7] C4 (English) is ~305GB of cleaned Common Crawl, but it lacks educational-quality filtering and contains noisier web text compared to FineWeb-Edu, making it less suitable for a tiny model that needs maximum signal per token.
- quote: "A colossal, cleaned version of Common Crawl's web crawl corpus... en: 305GB"
- url: https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/c4
- source_type: primary
- published: 2019-10-23
- confidence: high

### [8] The Pile (825 GiB, 22 diverse sub-datasets) is designed for large models; its diversity benefits models ≥1B params, but for a 30M model the token-per-domain ratio may be too diluted to learn coherent patterns from any single domain.
- quote: "The Pile is a 825 GiB diverse, open source language modelling data set that consists of 22 smaller, high-quality datasets combined together."
- url: https://pile.eleuther.ai/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2020-12-31
- confidence: high

### [9] Tiny language models have a hard capacity ceiling: a 30M-param model cannot store the factual knowledge or long-range dependencies that larger models learn, limiting it to local pattern matching and simple next-token prediction.
- quote: "LLM pre-training is currently feasible only for a few dominant companies due to the immense computational resources required, limiting broader research participation."
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.14871
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-07-20
- confidence: medium

### [10] Quality > quantity for tiny models: FineWeb-Edu's educational filtering means each of its ~10B tokens carries more learning signal than an equivalent volume from unfiltered web crawls like C4.
- quote: "FineWeb-Edu outperforms FineWeb on popular benchmarks and shows the power of classifiers trained on synthetic data."
- url: https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceFW/fineweb-edu
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-06-02
- confidence: high
