# F6: What do community training runs (nanoGPT, llm.c, Cerebras GPT) actually show for 10M–100M parameter GPT models trained on web data? Are there published loss curves, generated samples, or benchmark scores at this scale?

## Findings

### [1] Karpathy's llm.c reproduces GPT-2 (124M) at val loss 3.28 on 1.8B tokens of FineWeb
- quote: "It was a 124M GPT-2 architecture Transformer, on 10B tokens of FineWeb. The parameter count and the dataset token count matches the original 124M GPT-2."
- url: https://www.reddit.com/r/mlscaling/comments/1d3a793/andrej_karpathy_gpt2_124m_in_llmc_in_90_minutes/
- source_type: primary
- published: May 2024
- confidence: high

### [2] The nanoGPT speedrun target is val loss 3.28 cross-entropy on held-out FineWeb text
- quote: "The speedrun's goal is 3.28 average cross-entropy per token on held-out text. It is roughly equivalent to the model assigning the true next token 3.8% probability (in geometric-average terms)"
- url: https://nilmamano.com/blog/nanogpt-speedrun
- source_type: primary
- published: July 2026
- confidence: high

### [3] The nanoGPT speedrun model is 162M parameters (12 layers, 768 dim, 6 heads of 128)
- quote: "GPT(vocab_size=50304, num_layers=12, model_dim=768)... 162 million parameters stored as a mix of 16-bit and 32-bit floating-point numbers"
- url: https://nilmamano.com/blog/nanogpt-speedrun
- source_type: primary
- published: July 2026
- confidence: high

### [4] Training consumes 1.8B tokens (no repeats) with batch size 524,288 tokens per step over 3,500 steps
- quote: "Over 3,500 steps, that's 1.8B tokens, with no repeats; the model sees each training token once."
- url: https://nilmamano.com/blog/nanogpt-speedrun
- source_type: primary
- published: July 2026
- confidence: high

### [5] Speedrun record dropped from 45 minutes to under 90 seconds (step count from 19,560 to 2,690) on 8xH100
- quote: "Karpathy's version took 19,560 steps; this one took 3,500. The record is now at 2,690 (June 2026)."
- url: https://nilmamano.com/blog/nanogpt-speedrun
- source_type: primary
- published: July 2026
- confidence: high

### [6] Cerebras-GPT family starts at 111M parameters, trained on Eleuther Pile following Chinchilla scaling rules
- quote: "We introduce Cerebras-GPT, a family of open compute-optimal language models scaled from 111M to 13B parameters. We train Cerebras-GPT models on the Eleuther Pile dataset following DeepMind Chinchilla scaling rules"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03208
- source_type: primary (peer-reviewed)
- published: April 2023
- confidence: high

### [7] Cerebras-GPT shows predictable power-law scaling and state-of-the-art training efficiency at all sizes
- quote: "We characterize the predictable power-law scaling and compare Cerebras-GPT with other publicly-available models to show all Cerebras-GPT models have state-of-the-art training efficiency on both pre-training and downstream objectives"
- url: https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03208
- source_type: primary (peer-reviewed)
- published: April 2023
- confidence: high

### [8] nanoGPT includes a scaling_laws.ipynb that systematically varies model size from ~1.5M to ~600M parameters
- quote: "Model Size: Tiny (n_layer=4, n_head=4, n_embd=128, ~1.5M params), Small (n_layer=8, n_head=8, n_embd=256, ~12M params), Medium (n_layer=12, n_head=12, n_embd=512, ~80M params), Large (n_layer=24, n_head=16, n_embd=1024, ~600M params)"
- url: https://deepwiki.com/karpathy/nanogpt/7-scaling-laws-analysis
- source_type: primary
- published: April 2025
- confidence: medium

### [9] Muon optimizer (invented for the speedrun) reduced training steps by ~3x compared to AdamW at 162M scale
- quote: "Muon does this to gradient matrices... Equalizing the strengths lets every useful direction make progress at once. That is Muon's bet, and empirically it pays off: it reduced the number of steps by roughly a factor of 3."
- url: https://nilmamano.com/blog/nanogpt-speedrun
- source_type: primary
- published: July 2026
- confidence: high

### [10] Speedrun uses multiple ML tricks proven at 162M scale: document alignment, dynamic attention windows, RoPE, QK-norm, ReLU^2, logit soft-capping
- quote: "Many of the improvements in the last 20% have not yet been published outside of the modded-nanogpt repo... Document Alignment (3s impact), Dynamic Attention Window (4s impact), Heterogenous Batch Sizes (4s impact), Backout (2s impact)"
- url: https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/j3gp8tebQiFJqzBgg/how-the-nanogpt-speedrun-wr-dropped-by-20-in-3-months
- source_type: community
- published: October 2025
- confidence: high

### [11] microgpt demonstrates the complete training loop: loss drops from 3.3 (random) to 2.37 on a 4K-param model in 1,000 steps
- quote: "Over 1,000 steps the loss decreases from around 3.3 (random guessing among 27 tokens: -log(1/27) ≈ 3.3) down to around 2.37."
- url: https://karpathy.github.io/2026/02/12/microgpt/
- source_type: primary
- published: February 2026
- confidence: high
